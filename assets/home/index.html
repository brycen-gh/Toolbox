A little help getting started

FIELD GUIDE / 01

First time here?

Open the service your instructor assigned. Each application has its own login. Ask your instructor for your account or invitation; this portal does not store credentials.

A service won't open?

Check you are connected to the lab network. Ask your instructor to check the container and published port in Toolbox. A service may still be starting. For HTTPS certificate messages, confirm the address and certificate with your instructor before proceeding.

Service guide: courses, projects, notes, files & challenges

Moodle — courses & activities: sign in with your assigned account, then open your course and activities to follow your training progress.

OpenProject — projects & tasks: sign in with your assigned account and open your project to find assigned work and track team progress.

Etherpad — shared notes: use the pad name or link supplied for your group to write together in real time.

Nextcloud — files & resources: sign in to access your lab files and shared resources.

CTFd — challenges & flags: follow your instructor's registration instructions, then open Challenges to submit flags and view the scoreboard.

Zulip — team conversations: use your instructor's invitation, sign in, and open the channels and topics assigned to your group.

Portainer — container management: instructors can sign in to inspect containers, review logs, and manage lab services.

Portainer & Zulip first-time setup

Instructors: Portainer's first-time setup requires your configured setup token and a new administrator password. Find the token in Toolbox's Portainer settings or container logs. For Zulip, use Deployment → Containers → Zulip → Create Organization and Admin, or use Zulip Create Organization Link for browser setup, then invite students. These setup credentials and private links are intentionally absent from this portal.

Zulip's external hostname must resolve on client computers. OpenProject's configured hostname and Nextcloud's trusted domains must also match the address students use.

Capture and replay lab traffic

In the desktop Toolbox, open Capture → Capture Traffic. Select the Ethernet or Wi-Fi adapter, choose a new .pcap file, and start. Stop Capture finishes the file. Wi-Fi monitor mode is not enabled.

For replay, put captures in repos/pcap, configure the Training menu entry and select TARGET_DEVICE under Training → Settings → Variable Settings. Replay is local, even when SecOnion is remote. WSL adapters may be virtual; use Linux with direct access to the adapter for physical Ethernet replay. Replay only on the assigned training network.

Instructor maintenance & offline use

Use Toolbox's Container Actions to read logs, restart a service, or edit its saved settings. Back up service data before upgrades or resets. Changed deployment settings require recreation; restarting alone does not apply them.

Home works without internet after its image is loaded. Update service ports or the portal hostname in Toolbox, enable HOME_RECREATE, and run Install Home again to regenerate links. Students need network access to the portal and each service's port.
